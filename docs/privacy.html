Privacy Policy

Daily Brief is a personal publishing tool that posts news video reports to the operator's own YouTube, Facebook, Instagram and TikTok accounts.

It does not collect, store or sell data about viewers or other users. The only account it signs in to is the operator's own, and the sign-in tokens it receives are used solely to upload videos and read view counts for those videos. Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets and are never shared.

News content is summarised from public news sources, which are credited in each post. Images are openly licensed and credited.

Questions: open an issue at github.com/augustinendubuisicharles-cell/contentbot.
